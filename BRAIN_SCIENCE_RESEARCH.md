### Therapeutic Music Parameters

| Parameter | Recommendation | Reason |
|-----------|----------------|--------|
| Tempo | 60-80 BPM | Matches resting heart rate, induces calm |
| Dynamics | Consistent, no sudden peaks | Prevents startle response in trauma |
| Duration | 3-5 minutes | Matches attention spans in distress |
| Lyrics | Hope-centered, non-triggering | Supports cognitive reframing |
| Keys | Minor for acknowledgment, major for resolution | Emotional congruence |

### Thought Detective Validation

| Distortion | CBT Research | TWG Implementation |
