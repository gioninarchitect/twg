## Introduction Email (Emotional, Attention-Grabbing)

**Subject:** From Africa to Your Inbox: Your 12 Principles Are Reaching Women Who Have No Psychiatrists

---

Dr. Amen,

I'm writing from South Africa, where we have **1 psychiatrist for every 100,000 people**.

In our townships, women carry the weight of generations. Domestic violence. HIV. Absent fathers. Economic desperation. The trauma is layered so deep it feels like inheritance.

They will never see a SPECT scan. They will never sit in your Costa Mesa clinic. But they have smartphones. And they have faith.

**That's where you come in.**

I've been studying your work - particularly your 12 Principles - and I realized something: your message isn't meant to stay inside American clinics. It's meant to reach the woman in Soweto who thinks her anxiety is a spiritual attack. The grandmother in Lagos raising her grandchildren through her own unprocessed grief. The young professional in Nairobi who masks her depression because "African women are strong."

**We're building something called Tea With God.**

It's a 40-day mobile app that takes your brain health principles and wraps them in culturally relevant, faith-integrated exercises that work offline, on low-end phones, in the hands of women who've never heard the word "neuroplasticity" but desperately need its promise:

*"You are not stuck with the brain you have. You can make it better."*

We're calling them "Brain Games" - but they're really your principles made accessible:
- Breathing exercises based on Polyvagal Theory
- Gratitude journaling from Positive Psychology
- Cognitive reframing from CBT
- Memory training for scripture (so faith strengthens the brain)
- Body awareness for those carrying trauma in their bodies

**We're not therapists. We're not pretending to be.**

Every screen reminds them: this is educational, not treatment. If you're in crisis, here's a number. We're simply creating a bridge - between suffering in silence and finding hope - for women who will never access clinical care.

**Here's what I'm asking:**

I would be honored to share what we're building with you. Not to steal your work - to extend it. To bring your message to a continent that needs it desperately.

If your 12 Principles could reach 1 million African women through an app they can use while riding a taxi, cooking dinner, or lying awake at 2am... would you want to know about it?

I'm not asking for money. I'm asking for 15 minutes of your attention.

If this resonates, I'd love to send you a demo. If it doesn't, I understand - I'm just a stranger from Africa in your inbox.

But I had to try. Because the women I'm thinking of don't have a Dr. Amen. They have a phone, a faith, and a hope that someone somewhere cares about their healing.

Thank you for reading this far.

With deep respect,
