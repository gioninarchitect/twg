Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our B2B plans

What data can our organization see about members?

Privacy is paramount. Your organization dashboard shows aggregate metrics only: total active users, average progress day, phase completion rates, and general engagement. You will never see individual journal entries, personal notes, or specific emotional data.

Can we customize the app with our branding?

Our Ministry Plus and Enterprise tiers include white-label options. You can add your organization's logo to the welcome screen, customize the primary color scheme, and add a personalized welcome message for your members.

How do access codes work?

When you purchase a plan, you receive unique access codes to distribute to your members. Each member downloads the Tea With God app and enters their code to unlock their access level. Codes can only be used once and are linked to the member's device.

What happens if we need more codes?

You can purchase additional codes at any time at the same per-user rate. Enterprise customers can also upgrade to unlimited codes. Contact us to add more codes to your existing plan.

Is there a free trial for organizations?

Yes! We offer a 14-day pilot program with 10 free Premium access codes for qualifying organizations. This allows your leadership team to experience the journey firsthand before committing to a full rollout.

Why is pricing different in different countries?

We believe healing tools should be accessible regardless of economic circumstances. Our pricing is adjusted based on local purchasing power, not direct currency conversion. This means a church in Nigeria pays what's affordable in Nigeria, not a US-dollar equivalent that might be out of reach.
