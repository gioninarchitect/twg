Privacy & Data

Absolutely. Your journal entries are stored only on your device and are encrypted. We cannot read your entries - they never leave your phone. Your healing journey is completely private.
Since your journal and progress are stored locally, deleting the app will remove this data from your device. Your access code remains valid, so you can reinstall and start fresh. We recommend exporting any journal entries you want to keep before uninstalling.

The Journey

No pressure, no demands. While the journey is designed to be experienced one day at a time, you can move at your own pace. Some days you might need to sit longer. Other days you might move forward. This is your journey.
These are gentle, evidence-based exercises designed to support your healing. They include breathing exercises, gratitude practices, thought reframing, and scripture memory tools - all grounded in psychology and faith.
Tea With God is a devotional companion, not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're struggling with serious mental health issues, please seek help from a qualified therapist or counselor. This app is designed to complement, not replace, professional support.
