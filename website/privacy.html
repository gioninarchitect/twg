Privacy Policy

Your data stays private. Your journey stays sacred.

Last updated: January 2025

Our Promise: Tea With God is built on the principle of privacy by design. Your journal entries, reflections, and personal data never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to back them up.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide you with a meaningful experience:

  • Account Information: Email address and name when you purchase access
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through Yoco - we never store your card details
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics to improve the app (no personal content is tracked)

What Stays on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Journal entries and personal reflections
  • Voice recordings
  • Progress through the 40-day journey
  • Brain game results and personal insights
  • Any notes or highlights you create

Data Security

Your privacy is protected through multiple layers of security:

  • Journal entries are encrypted on your device
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all communications
  • No third-party analytics that track personal content
  • Payment processing through PCI-compliant Yoco

Your Rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Access any personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

Children's Privacy

Tea With God is designed for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
